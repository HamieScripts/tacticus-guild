# Tacticus Guild

Terms used to describe guild activity and results in Tacticus.

## Language

**Live War Capture**:
A snapshot of an in-progress Guild War taken from the Current War endpoint.

**War Archive**:
The final record of a completed Guild War, captured from the Recent War endpoint before the next war ends. Once saved, it does not change.

**War Battle**:
A single Guild War attack attempt by either guild that reached a recorded outcome, including an abandoned attempt. Zone claims, target marks, and other non-battle activity are not battles.
